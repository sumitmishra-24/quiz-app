📋 Quiz Master Rules

Read the instructions before starting your quiz!

🎯 1. Quiz Overview

  • The question bank contains 50 questions.
  • Questions cover HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Each question has a 15-second time limit.

📚 2. Categories & Difficulty

  • Select a category to practise a specific topic.
  • Choose All Difficulties to include every difficulty level.
  • Questions and answer options appear in random order.

✅ 3. Answering Rules

  • Each question has four answer options.
  • Each correct answer earns one point.
  • You cannot change your answer after selecting it.
  • If time runs out, the correct answer is revealed.

🏆 4. Scores & History

  • Your final score and percentage appear at the end.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.
  • You can view previous attempts in Quiz History.

⚙️ 5. Additional Features

  • Switch between Light Mode and Dark Mode.
  • Turn sound effects on or off.
  • Use Back to Setup to leave an ongoing quiz.
